PolyBot Archive

Privacy policy

Effective November 11, 2026

PolyBot Archive is a personal desktop utility operated by its owner to archive selected files in the owner's Google Drive. It is not offered as a public multi-user service.

Data accessed and purpose

The uploader accesses selected archive files, restoration metadata, and file checksums to transfer and verify backups. It reads the authorized Google account identity and storage quota to confirm that the intended account has sufficient capacity.

Google Drive access uses the limited drive.file scope, covering files created by or explicitly selected for this application. The application does not request unrestricted access to every file in the account.

Storage and protection

Files are stored in the owner's Google Drive. Local originals, manifests, and verification records may also be retained on the owner's computer. Authorization credentials are stored separately in access-restricted local configuration and are not included in archives or this public website. Transfers use encrypted HTTPS connections.

Sharing and use

The uploader does not sell data, serve advertisements, or deliberately make archived files public. It sends selected files to Google Drive solely for the owner's archive workflow. Archive contents are not used by the uploader to train artificial-intelligence models.

PolyBot Archive's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention, revocation, and deletion

The owner controls retention. Uploaded files and local archives remain until the owner removes them. Revoking access stops future authorized access but does not automatically delete existing copies.

The owner may revoke PolyBot Archive through Google Account connections, delete cloud copies in Google Drive, and remove local files and credentials from their computer.

This website

This static site has no application sign-in, upload form, advertising, or analytics scripts. GitHub Pages hosts the site and a GitHub-hosted icon. GitHub may process ordinary request information, such as IP addresses, under its privacy statement. No archive contents are hosted here.

Questions and changes

The application owner maintains this policy through the public information repository. Do not post credentials, private files, or account information in public issues. Material changes will be reflected in the effective date on this page.
